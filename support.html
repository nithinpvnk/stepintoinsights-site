Frequently asked

The app shows no data / zeros.

Step into Insights needs permission to read Apple Health. Grant access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Step into Insights and turn the categories on, then reopen the app. In the app, Settings → Health Data Status → Refresh permissions re-checks access.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Everything is analyzed on your iPhone and never leaves your device. See our Privacy Policy.

How do subscriptions and the free trial work?

Premium is available monthly or annually (with a free trial), or as a one-time lifetime purchase, through the App Store. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases in the app's Settings if a purchase doesn't appear.

Why does it ask for so many Health types?

Extra signals (heart rate, HRV, sleep, etc.) power the optional readiness, strain, and recovery insights. The app still works with just steps — anything you don't grant is simply skipped, never guessed.

How do I delete my data?

All app data lives on your device. Deleting the app removes it completely.
