Privacy Policy

Step into Insights · Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version: Step into Insights reads your Apple Health data on your Apple devices and never sends Health data to us. There is no account and no Step into Insights health-data server.

Who we are

Step into Insights ("the app," "we," "us") is an iOS app that turns your Apple Health activity into step, workout, and wellness insights. It is an independent app developed by Nithin Putchakayala.

Health & fitness data

With your permission, the app reads the following from Apple Health: steps, distance, active and resting energy, flights climbed, workouts and workout routes, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate recovery, walking and running speed, sleep, stand and exercise minutes, time in daylight, body mass, date of birth, and biological sex.

  • This data is read only — the app never writes to or modifies Apple Health.
  • All analysis happens on your device. Your health data is never transmitted to us, stored on a server, or shared with any third party.
  • Cached results (your daily snapshot, score history) are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, a copy of that storage is part of your backup until the backup itself is deleted.
  • You control access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Step into Insights.

Other information kept on your device

The app also stores your preferences (daily goal, units, display name, tone and notification settings), the things you create in it (saved insight stories, Walk Mode walks and recaps, your side-quest, challenge and badge progress, and, if you take them, goal-day snap photos, which stay in the app's private storage and leave your device only if you share one) and, if you choose to add one, a birthday. The birthday sets how high your level can climb and powers a birthday moment. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere, analytics included. To share progress with its widgets on the same device, the app saves a small snapshot of today's activity, the last seven days of step totals, and your level and journey standing. You can hide step counts from widgets in the app's Settings.

Not medical advice

Every score and number in the app (including Readiness, Recovery, Strain, Training Load, walking-calorie estimates and the daily brief) is a metric calculated from the data Apple Health has for you. These are estimates for general fitness and wellness interest only. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment, and they do not replace a doctor. If you have a health concern, or before you change how you exercise, talk to a qualified healthcare professional.

Motion & Fitness and camera

Walk Mode asks for Motion & Fitness access the first time you start a walk, to show a live step count on your iPhone. That live count is not stored anywhere but on your device; the walk's saved total still comes from Apple Health. The optional goal-day snap asks for camera access the first time you take one. Snap photos are saved only in the app's private storage (never your photo library) and leave your device only if you share one. The app does not use your location.

Analytics (optional, off by default)

To understand which features are used, the app can send anonymous, privacy-preserving usage events via TelemetryDeck. This is strictly opt-in: nothing is sent unless you explicitly turn analytics on.

  • No health data, no personal information, and no advertising identifiers are ever included.
  • TelemetryDeck uses a pseudonymous hashed installation identifier to group sessions. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used for tracking.
  • You can turn analytics off again at any time in the app's Settings.

The only information that ever reaches a server run by someone other than Apple is that optional analytics, and it contains event names and fixed option values only: for example which screen was opened or which setting was switched. It never contains Health values, names, birthdays, photos or free text.

Apple Watch and maps

If you enable Watch surfaces, the app sends a display snapshot directly to your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. Workout-route maps use Apple's system mapping services to render map content. Step into Insights does not upload HealthKit values to its own server or to TelemetryDeck.

Purchases

Optional premium features are sold as in-app purchases and subscriptions handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase validation is performed on-device through Apple's StoreKit.

Our App Store privacy answers and privacy manifest

The same facts are declared to Apple in the app's App Store privacy label and in its privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), and they match this page:

  • Tracking: none. No advertising identifier, no tracking domains, no data sold or shared.
  • Data not linked to you (analytics, only if you opt in): Product interaction and Device ID (the pseudonymous hashed installation identifier from TelemetryDeck).
  • Data linked to you: Purchase history, for app functionality, handled by Apple's App Store; we never see payment details.
  • Not collected: your Health and fitness data, location, contacts, photos, name, email or any account, because none of it leaves your device.

Notifications

If you enable them, reminders and celebration notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not operate a push-notification server and send nothing to you remotely.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or store your Health data or personal information on any server. The only usage data we receive is the optional analytics described above.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not require an account, email, or sign-in.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.
